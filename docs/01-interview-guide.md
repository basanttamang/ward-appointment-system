# Stakeholder Interview Guide

**Length:** 15–20 minutes · **Format:** in person or by phone
**Before you start:** explain the project is a personal study, ask permission to take notes, and say names will not be recorded (use Citizen A, Staff B…).

## Citizens

1. Which ward service did you use most recently? (e.g. recommendation letter, birth or death registration, relationship verification, migration certificate)
2. Walk me through that visit from the moment you decided to go.
3. How long did you wait? How many visits did it take to finish?
4. Were you asked for documents you didn't bring? How did you find out what was needed?
5. What was the most frustrating part?
6. Do you use a smartphone? Would you book a time online, by SMS, or would you rather just walk in?
7. If you could book a time, what would you want to know before you go?
8. Anything else that would have made it easier?

## Ward staff

1. Which services are busiest, and on which days and times?
2. Roughly how many people do you serve on a normal day? On the busiest day?
3. What causes delays at the desk? How often do people arrive with missing documents?
4. What records do you keep for each visitor or application? On paper, in Excel, or in a system?
5. Who asks you for reports, and what do they want to know?
6. If people booked a time, what would worry you? (e.g. no-shows, people without phones, walk-ins)
7. Who should be able to see or change a booking?
8. How should walk-in visitors be handled if a booking system existed?

## After each interview

Write up notes the same day in [02-interview-notes.md](02-interview-notes.md). List each pain point with an ID (P1, P2…) so requirements can trace back to it.
