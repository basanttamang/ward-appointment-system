# Project Charter

**Project:** Ward Office Appointment System
**Version:** 1.0 (baseline) · **Date:** 22 October 2026
**Project manager:** Basant Tamang

## 1. Objective

Specify, prototype and test an online appointment system for ward office services, so that citizens can book a time slot and know which documents to bring, and staff can see and manage each day's demand.

Success is measured by:

- A validated SRS with every requirement traced to a stakeholder need and a test
- A working prototype that passes at least 90% of acceptance scenarios in UAT
- Delivery by **2 November 2026**, with all project records complete

## 2. Scope

**In scope**

- Citizen booking of a slot for a ward service, by web on a mobile phone
- List of required documents per service
- Staff daily queue: mark arrived, completed or no-show
- Daily report of bookings by service and status
- Audit log of every status change
- Requirements, data model, API specification and acceptance testing

**Out of scope**

- Online payment of fees
- Issuing the actual document or recommendation letter online
- Integration with real national ID or government systems (specified as interfaces only)
- Live SMS sending (specified; simulated in the prototype)

## 3. Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Interest | How involved |
|---|---|---|

## 6. Top risks

| ID | Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Response |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Ward staff unavailable for interview | Medium | High | Interview citizens first; ask a contact to introduce; use published service lists |
| R2 | Volunteer team has limited time | High | Medium | Keep prototype scope small; prioritise must-have requirements |
| R3 | Scope grows (e.g. payments, SMS) | Medium | Medium | All changes go through a change request and decision log |
| R4 | Tight timeline to 2 Nov | High | High | Daily check-ins; cut should-have items first |
| R5 | Requirements based on assumptions | Medium | High | Mark assumed requirements; validate each with a stakeholder |

## 7. Governance

- Check-in every two days (15 minutes); weekly status report each Friday
- The project manager decides within the agreed scope; changes to scope, dates or must-have requirements are decided with the stakeholder and recorded in the decision log
- Acceptance is confirmed in writing by a stakeholder after UAT
